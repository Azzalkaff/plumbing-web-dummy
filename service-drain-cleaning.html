Drain Cleaning

Clear slow or blocked drains in sinks, showers, tubs, toilets, and main lines.

Slow drains, standing water, and recurring clogs are usually signs of a blockage that needs more than a quick fix. We clear blockages in sinks, showers, tubs, toilets, and main drain lines.

We look for the cause of the blockage so we can recommend the right solution, whether that is a one-time clearing or a repair to the pipe itself.

What is included

  • Kitchen sink drain clearing
  • Shower and tub drain clearing
  • Toilet clog removal
  • Main drain line clearing
  • Camera drain inspection
  • Preventive drain maintenance

How it works

  1. Inspect

    We locate the blockage and check the condition of the affected drain.

  2. Clear

    We clear the blockage with equipment suited to the type of drain and clog.

  3. Test the flow

    We run water to confirm the drain flows freely.

  4. Advise

    We explain the likely cause and how to avoid it happening again.

Common questions

Slow drains are usually caused by a buildup of grease, hair, soap, or debris. Left alone, a partial blockage can become a full one.

Chemical cleaners can damage some pipes and may not clear the whole blockage. If a drain keeps clogging, it is better to have it inspected.

Keep grease and food scraps out of kitchen drains, use hair catchers in showers, and avoid flushing anything other than toilet paper.
